My name is Elie. I’m from France, working on long-term problems across health and technology.

This blog is a working log ; thoughts, progress, mistakes, and course corrections. A record of what it takes to keep going.


## My future

My goal is simple: do useful work, stay honest, and one day find myself in the Alaskan wilderness with huskies.
